The founder

Dharanya Sundar

A Costume Design graduate with a natural artist's eye and a lifelong love of crafts, curating and design. Dharanya founded Giftloom Studio on a simple belief: a gift should not end when the box is opened.

"It should linger, like a fragrance, a texture, a small smile."

Her story

From costume design to the craft of gifting.

Costume design is the discipline of telling a story through fabric, colour and detail, and of making every piece feel right for the person who wears it. Dharanya brings the same instinct to gifting. She starts with the people who will open the box, and only then chooses what goes inside.

That is why Giftloom Studio begins every project with a conversation about the occasion, the people and the feeling, rather than a product list. Colours are matched, textures are balanced and branding is placed with restraint, so a corporate gift feels personal instead of promotional.

We would rather craft a moment than sell a hamper.

As the studio has grown to serve companies, conferences, hotels, hospitals and schools, one rule has not changed. Orders may come in hundreds, but each box is still handled one at a time: arranged by hand, checked, ribboned, carded and packed with care before it leaves the studio.

Today Giftloom Studio curates four catalogues and nineteen collections, from leather desk accessories and branded calendars to Tamil Nadu sweets, artisan heritage keepsakes, spa amenities and newborn kits, and weaves them into gifts that people keep.
